# The Story: The Broken Link (6-part Series)
# Style: Detailed Stickman / Simple Comic Style

# Image 1: The Attrition
Detailed stickman and simple comic art style, flat desaturated colors in drab browns, grays, and muted earth tones, thick clean bold black outlines, 2D graphic novel illustration. Scene: A harsh daytime death march across cracked barren earth toward a distant blocky city on the horizon. In the center, viewed from slightly behind, a young boy with messy dark brown hair, bare feet, and a tattered gray-blue tunic walks with head slightly down but eyes sharply focused on an armored guard to his right. Oversized heavy iron chain links bind the captives together. To the right, a faceless guard in a dark grey cloak and rounded iron helmet with dark visor drags his triangular-tipped spear, favoring a wounded limp leg. Grim survival tone, wide angle.
