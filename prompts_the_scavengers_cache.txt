# The Story: The Scavenger's Cache (10-part Series)
# Style: Detailed Stickman / Simple Comic Style

# Image 1: The Canyon Descent
Detailed stickman and simple comic art style, flat desaturated colors in drab browns, grays, and muted earth tones, thick clean bold black outlines, 2D graphic novel illustration. Scene: A steep, jagged canyon wall. The young boy with messy dark brown hair and torn gray-blue tunic is carefully rappelling down a crumbling rock face using a fraying hemp rope. The iron guard spear is strapped diagonally across his back. Deep canyon floor shrouded in morning mist below, cracked dry earth. Tactical descent, dynamic angle looking downward.
